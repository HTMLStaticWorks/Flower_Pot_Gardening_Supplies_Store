The Ultimate Watering Guide

Overwatering is the #1 killer of houseplants. Master the art of hydration with these foolproof steps.

1
Check the Soil, Not a Schedule

Always check moisture levels 2-3 inches deep before watering. If it's dry, water. If it's damp, wait.

2
Water Deeply

Water thoroughly until it drains out the bottom holes. This encourages deep, healthy root systems.

3
Empty the Drip Tray

Never let your plant sit in standing water for more than 30 minutes to prevent root rot.

Fertilizing Demystified

Plants need food just like we do. Learn how to read N-P-K ratios and when to feed your collection.

Nutrient Function
N (Nitrogen) Promotes lush, green leafy growth.
P (Phosphorus) Encourages strong root development and blooming.
K (Potassium) Aids overall plant health and disease resistance.

Pro Tip: Only fertilize during the active growing season (Spring/Summer). Dilute liquid fertilizers to half-strength for indoor plants.
