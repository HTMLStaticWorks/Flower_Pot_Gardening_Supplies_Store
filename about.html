Our Story

Rooted in passion, growing with purpose. Discover the journey behind GreenNest.

Since 2018

From a Small Seed to a Thriving Community

GreenNest started in a small backyard greenhouse with a simple mission: to make premium quality gardening accessible to urban dwellers.

We realized that finding stylish, modern planters that also provided proper drainage and care for plants was incredibly difficult. What began as a personal project to design the perfect ceramic pot quickly blossomed into a full-scale operation.
