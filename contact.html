Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about shipping, returns, and plant care.

All live plants are carefully hand-packaged using our proprietary eco-friendly wrap that secures the soil and protects the foliage. During extreme weather, we include heat packs or cooling gel packs at no extra charge to ensure your plant arrives healthy.

We offer a 30-Day Plant Guarantee. If your plant arrives damaged or dies within 30 days despite proper care, we will replace it for free. Hard goods like pots and tools can be returned within 60 days in unused condition.

Currently, we only ship live plants within the contiguous United States due to agricultural regulations. However, our pots, tools, and accessories can be shipped to Canada and the UK.

Orders are processed quickly to ensure healthy plant delivery. You can cancel or modify your order within 2 hours of placement. Please contact our support team immediately if changes are needed.
